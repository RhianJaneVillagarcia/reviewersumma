What did we use?

  • Internal CSS: the style tag
  • Selectors: body, h1, p (element) and .card (class)
  • Text properties: text-align, text-transform, font-size
  • Gradient: linear-gradient on the body and the card
  • Division tag: div with class="card"
  • Opacity: opacity: 0.9;
